READ THIS BEFORE SELECTING

General quality carries the signal.

The selected rating pulls small pressure samples toward each player's all-situation quality. The data did not reveal a separate pressure-specific component. Pressure occurs mostly in chases (41.1% of second-innings balls; 0.6% of first-innings balls). The analysis pools IPL and men's T20Is, and intervals do not include uncertainty in the win-probability model.
